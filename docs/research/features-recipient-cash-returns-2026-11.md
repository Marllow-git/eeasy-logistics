# Feature census: recipient experience, cash and COD finance, returns and claims (2026-11)

Date: 2026-11-02. Author: researcher agent. Branch: `docs/features-recipient-cash-returns`. Status: research input for the lead; no decisions taken here.
Repo baseline: `origin/main` at `1e0c184`. Roadmap and architecture facts are read from `docs/roadmap.md` §1 and §4 to §5, `docs/architecture/domain-model.md` §3.1, §3.10 to §3.16, §6.3, and `docs/architecture/integrations.md` §2.9, §5.1 to §5.5.

## Summary for the lead

1. Covered in the roadmap: the tracking page (DEL-8, WEB-8), WhatsApp status templates (INT-3, wave 1), reschedule and address-pin links (DEL-14, WEB-11, wave 2), opt-out (INT-3, integrations §5.5), Arabic and English templates, split COD and custody (DEL-7, DEL-11, DEL-12), and failed-to-returned return to origin (domain §3.13).
2. Cash custody has the same stage model that Shipox describes (to be collected, with driver, with cashier, with bank). Our model is stricter (append-only, PIN, cash block D-11). Missing: a merchant-facing settlement statement, finance exports, a COD reversal path, and per-order reconciliation.
3. Card and mada at the door are recorded as `not_held` (D-18) but not processed. SoftPOS, payment links and digital COD all sit behind ADR-0017 (owner gate) and have no order.
4. Returns: return pickup and exchange are one wave-4 order (DEL-18); dispositions into stock are WMS-8 (wave 4). Claims for damage or loss, customer disputes and refund triggers have no order and no aggregate in domain §3 or §4.
5. Recipient gaps versus the benchmarked set: branded tracking (integrations §5.1 says merchant branding is not available), delivery instructions, ratings and feedback, a recipient-initiated return request, and a recipient-side photo or signature view (D-24 (domain §3.18) shows status, ETA, driver first name and position only).
6. Two internal inconsistencies for the lead: (a) the wave-1 `order_out_for_delivery` template carries an ETA window, but ETA depends on INT-6 (wave 2) and slots (DEL-9, wave 2); (b) `recipient_otp` is a proof kind (domain §3.10) with no recipient OTP template in integrations §5.1.
7. Regional benchmarks read: Salla COD fee is set by the carrier (SMSA SAR 8 plus 2 percent, Aramex SAR 8 plus 1 percent, per Salla); Salla pays out on Sundays and Wednesdays; mada refunds must be made within 30 days; COD is hidden below SAR 20 on Salla. These are secondary-source figures for the lead to verify before pricing decisions.
8. Compliance flag: our quiet hours are 22:00 to 08:00 (integrations §5.1 and §5.4). A vendor guide reports a marketing ban from 20:00 to 08:00 in KSA, and a law-firm note reports a 24-hour opt-out window. Neither was checked against the regulator. Needs counsel.
9. Evidence is thin for Torod, Tookan, Bringg, FarEye, Narvar, SMSA, Naqel, Neoleap and Shipox returns. Several rows rest on search summaries or vendor connector pages; these are marked in the source list.
10. Proposed orders and owner questions are at the end (ten proposals and fourteen questions).

## Method and how to read the tables

- Each feature cites source IDs from the registry at the end. Each registry row gives the URL, the date read (2026-11-02 for all) and the mode: **fetched** (page loaded and summarised), **search** (only the search-result summary was available), or **vendor** (a third-party vendor page selling a connector or plugin).
- Statements about what a product does come from the source. Statements marked "(inferred)" are this researcher's inference and are not in any source.
- Relevance is a judgement: high means the feature is central to a KSA or GCC merchant running COD and recipient messages on WhatsApp; medium means it appears in several regional offers but is not universal; low means no regional evidence was found or the feature assumes a model we do not run (for example carrier labels for own-fleet deliveries).
- Roadmap coverage: "covered" names the order and wave that delivers it; "partial" names the order and what is missing; "missing" means no order in roadmap §1 names it.
- Suggested wave is the lead's decision; the value shown is this researcher's proposal for the lead to accept or reject.

## Proposed orders

Each proposal is one line. Dependencies name roadmap order IDs. The lead decides the IDs and waves.

1. **Recipient OTP at the door** (amend DEL-7, INT-3, DRV-4): add the `recipient_otp` template and the driver step; depends on DEL-7, INT-3, DRV-4. Wave 1.
2. **Delivery instructions and access notes** (new order): optional instruction field on the order and recipient, shown to the driver and on reschedule and pin pages; depends on DEL-2, DEL-14, DRV-4. Wave 2.
3. **COD reversal path** (new order): a custody entry type that reverses a collection on return or cancellation after collection, append-only; depends on DEL-11, DEL-18. Wave 2 for the entry type, wave 4 for the return flow.
4. **Cash check-in screen for returns** (amend WEB-5 or add to DEL-18): scan-in of returned parcels with condition; depends on DEL-2, DRV-4. Wave 2.
5. **Customer dispute record** (new order): dispute entity linked to proofs and events, with statuses and outcome; depends on DEL-7, DEL-8, INT-3. Wave 2 or 3.
6. **Damage and loss claims with evidence** (new order): claim entity with evidence (photos, value, POD reference), status, and link to driver liability where ruled; depends on DEL-7, DEL-11, DEL-18. Wave 3.
7. **Recipient return request** (new order, pulls part of DEL-18 forward): return purpose on TrackingToken, reason list, approval state, pickup booking; depends on DEL-8, DEL-18, INT-3. Wave 4 unless ruled earlier.
8. **Branded tracking** (new order, if ruled in): merchant logo and colours on the recipient page and in templates; depends on WEB-8, ADR-0033, Q-21. Wave 2 or 3.
9. **Merchant COD statement and finance export** (new read-only order): statement and CSV or Excel exports over the cash ledgers; depends on DEL-11, DEL-12, Q-32. Wave 2 or 3.
10. **Ratings and feedback** (new order): post-delivery survey through INT-3 with consent checks, rating stored with the order; depends on DEL-8, INT-3, PLAT-9. Wave 3.

## Unanswered

1. Do we accept SMS before wave 3 for recipients without WhatsApp, given ADR-0037? Competitors send SMS and WhatsApp (S14, S17).
2. Is the recipient OTP at the door a wave-1 feature? Domain §3.10 has the kind; integrations §5.1 has no template.
3. May the recipient page and templates carry the merchant's logo and colours? integrations §5.1 says no; Shipday and Torod offer it (S6, S14).
4. Should a recipient-initiated return request come before wave 4? OTO and Torod list it (S6, S9).
5. Which ETA source is accepted for wave 1: slot windows (DEL-9 is wave 2) or an interim estimate? INT-6 is wave 2.
6. Are pay-later providers (Tabby, Tamara) in scope as COD alternatives? No source read gives KSA evidence of merchant demand.
7. Does the merchant need a COD statement and payout when it holds the cash itself under own-fleet rules? Salla and Torod pay out to merchants because they are carriers (S1, S5).
8. Does Eeasy set COD fees or COD limits (minimum cart, maximum value), or does the merchant? Salla uses SAR 20 and SAR 1,000 (S1).
9. Who records a refund and a COD reversal: Eeasy custody entries, the merchant's system through a webhook, or both? And is store credit in scope?
10. Are walk-in returns to the merchant's store in scope?
11. Are damage and loss costs charged to drivers through DriverLiabilityEntry? This is an HR and legal question as well as a data one.
12. Compliance: confirm the KSA marketing and messaging hours and the opt-out timing against the PDPL and CST texts. Our quiet hours are 22:00 to 08:00 (integrations §5.1); a vendor guide reports a 20:00 to 08:00 marketing window (S41); a law-firm note reports 24 hours for opt-out (S39). None is verified against the regulator.
13. Consent for feedback surveys: is a survey a transactional or a marketing message under PDPL? Needed before the ratings order.
14. ADR-0017 ruling on digital COD: which SoftPOS and payment-link provider (Geidea, Nearpay, or a bank SoftPOS)? Neoleap had no source in this census and needs a separate search. Nearpay pages disagree on the Android minimum (Android 9 on the Saudi page, S29; Android 10 on the global page, S30).
