# Feature census: orders and intake, zones and dispatch, hubs and linehaul (2026-11)

- **Dates:** every product page and doc below was read on 2026-11-02 unless marked "search summary" (a web search snippet, page not opened) or "failed" (the fetch returned a TLS, certificate or 404 error).
- **Repo baseline:** `docs/roadmap.md` §0 and §1 and `docs/architecture/domain-model.md` §3 and §4 on `main` at commit `1e0c184`, read 2026-11-02.
- **Labels:** [S] = the source states it. [I] = my inference from [S] facts. "Not named" = the page read did not name the feature; that is not proof the product lacks it.
- **Coverage column:** starts with `Covered`, `Partial` or `Missing`; `Excluded` marks a feature the owner rule rules out. Sources are in section 5 (S1 to S33).
- **Not a recommendation of architecture.** Waves follow `docs/roadmap.md` §1.

- [S] KSA-specific gaps with the highest relevance: bulk label printing for own-fleet orders (missing), COD eligibility rules per account (missing), pickup scheduling (partial: `pickup_request` kind only), address prompts for missing parts and district (partial), batching and vehicle or ability matching (missing).

## 6. Proposed orders

- **P1 Intake hardening (wave 1).** COD eligibility per account (cart value, weight, excluded items) and missing-part and district prompts at intake. Depends on DEL-2 and INT-2.
- **P2 Pickup scheduling (wave 2).** Recurring and cut-off-time pickup runs for the `pickup_request` kind. Depends on DEL-2, DEL-6 and DEL-9.
- **P3 Order labels and bulk actions (wave 1 or 2).** Printable order labels for own-fleet orders and bulk select actions on the orders list. Depends on DEL-2, WEB-5 and INT-1 (storage for label files).
- **P4 Batching and ability matching (wave 2).** Batch nearby orders into one run and match vehicle kind or ability tags, with an escalation rule for orders no driver accepts. Depends on DEL-10 and DEL-4.
- **P5 Time windows in the route plan (wave 2).** Customer windows and priorities as constraints in INT-6, and a per-stop ETA delay alert. Depends on INT-6 and DEL-9; needs the Q-18 ruling.
- **P6 Re-solve on change (owner decision on timing).** Pull the stop-change re-solve from DEL-16 (wave 4) to follow INT-6. Depends on INT-6 and DEL-10.
- **P7 Inter-site transfer (wave 4 or owner ruling).** Transfer state for orders between sites, with an arrival scan at the destination and a per-driver handover. Depends on DEL-6, DEL-7, DRV-4 and PLAT-12.
- **P8 Manifests and linehaul trips (wave 4 or later).** Outbound trip manifests, inbound manifest reconciliation and load balancing across vehicles. Depends on P7 and DEL-5.
- **P9 Hub capacity and sorting (later, owner question).** Dock throughput limits and sort-by-destination. Depends on WMS-1 and P8.
- **P10 Item hazard and temperature flags (owner scope decision).** Hazardous and cold-chain flags on items, with routing and vehicle rules. Depends on DEL-2 and the owner ruling in section 7.
