## 0. Goal

A platform admin signs in to the console, provisions an account from a sectioned form (name, locale, limits, modules, owner invitation) and watches it turn `active` when the owner accepts; lists and searches accounts; opens one and, under an "Acting on account" banner with a support reason, reads its members and audit log, sets its feature flags and suspends, reactivates or closes it with a reason; a super admin sets rollout modes; an operator inspects the outbox, reads a row's envelope and error, replays one dead row or a filtered set with a count confirm; a super admin invites staff, changes roles, revokes and resets a second factor; everyone with the permission reads the platform audit log with the support reason on cross-tenant rows; a staff member whose factor was reset re-enrols from the emailed link; every control is gated by `platformGetMe` and every screen works in Arabic and English with the census proving it against the real API, so that the wave 0 exit runs end to end in two browsers.

### 2a. Lead's improvements over the roadmap row

- **Close from `provisioned`** (PLAT-13 fix-round L-3) is on the screen, so an account whose owner never accepted does not need a suspension first.
- **Reason bounds differ by door**: 200 in the header, 500 in the body (PLAT-13 8.2 #33); the dialogs carry the right bound each, and the body dialogs prefill from the tab's reason so an operator types it once.
- **No totals anywhere** (ADR-0029): the home's only counts come from `platformListConsumers`, which is per consumer and bounded, not a `COUNT(*)`.
- **Bulk replay gated on an account filter** in the UI, which PLAT-8's M-1 made the API rule, with the reason stated on the disabled control instead of a `400` after the click.
- **Role change ends the session**: M-1 makes any token stale on the next call; the console treats `401 token_invalid` as a session end everywhere rather than as an error toast, and the role-change confirm tells the operator the person is signed out.
- **The re-enrolment link page** is here, not a later fix order: without it `platformResetStaffTotp` leaves a staff member unable to sign in.
- **The support reason survives a reload in its tab** (`sessionStorage`) so a refresh under the banner does not ask twice; it still never crosses tabs, which keeps §3.5's intent; the lead amends §3.5.
- **Census seeds a dead outbox row and a suspended account**, so the replay and reactivation paths run against real state instead of being skipped as "not reproducible".

## 5. Exit criteria

- The wave 0 exit runs in the census: a platform admin provisions an account, the owner accepts and the account is `active`; a support user reads the account's members under a banner with a reason, and the platform audit log shows that reason.
- Every control is gated by `platformGetMe` and every refusal renders the catalogue message; a `support` user never sees a write control; a role change signs the person out on their next call.
- Replay of one row and of a filtered set works against the seeded dead row with a reason and a confirm; no replay is one click.
- A staff member whose factor was reset re-enrols from the emailed link and signs in.
- Budgets hold; the Arabic release note exists; the Arabic screenshots of every screen are reviewed in §8.0.

The console sends such a reason percent-encoded (UTF-8) and counts the 200 bound on the encoded form; the API stores it encoded until it decodes the header.

8. **Accounts' short number.** `Account` has no number: the header and list show the last eight characters of the id (a UUIDv7 starts with its time, so census accounts share their first eight). Owner invitations in the header (2.5) are not shown: `Account` carries none and the members list needs the reason (leg 2's Members tab).
