# PLAT-12 · Accounts, members and sites

- **Status:** planned; brief after PLAT-11 closes (this order calls PLAT-11's `Invitations.createStaff`, `SecondFactorResets.issueLink`, `SessionService` revocations and the `core_memberships` table).
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Wave:** 0 (roadmap §1.1, critical path). **Depends on:** PLAT-10 (merged, PR #71), PLAT-11 (PR #83, merging). **Unblocks:** PLAT-13, WEB-3; DEL-4 (drivers reuse the membership writes).
- **Lead:** architecture lead. **Executor:** `executor-api` (Opus), own worktree, native stack scratch databases, Redis, Mailpit. **Adversary:** yes (authorization and visibility rules: owner-only grants, last owner, self-revocation, site scoping of `site_ids`; member data is personal data; identity §9 items 2, 3, 5).
- **Issue:** #86. **Merge danger:** one-way door (migration 0014 completes `core_accounts` and creates `core_sites` and `core_usage_counters`). **Legs:** two (A–D accounts and sites, E–H members).
- **Paths:** `apps/api` (new `modules/kernel/accounts/`, `modules/kernel/members/`, `modules/kernel/sites/`, `modules/kernel/platform/` repositories, `modules/kernel/public`), `packages/contracts` (scenarios only; the operations exist), `docs/architecture/data.md` (column notes on `core_accounts`, A-26 rows if the catalogue drifts), `docs/contracts/errors.md` (nothing expected), `.serena/memories`. The WEB-2 order in flight touches `apps/web` and `packages/ui` only.

## 0. Goal

An owner or admin reads their account with its limits and usage, changes its settings (intake rule, attempts, proof policy, reason codes, second-factor requirement) with a before-and-after audit row, invites staff by email into roles and sites, changes and revokes memberships under the separation-of-duties and owner rules, resets a member's second factor, and manages the sites that dispatch, cash custody and stock will hang off (ADR-0018), with every list paged by cursor (ADR-0029), every write idempotent, every row under RLS, and every site limit enforced, so that PLAT-13 provisions real accounts, WEB-3 has its settings screens, and DEL and WMS orders reference sites that exist.

## 1. Binding rules and ADRs

- AGENTS.md hard rules: tenancy from the token (`account_id` comes from the principal; no body or query names an account); side effects through the outbox (the invitation mail leaves through PLAT-11's `Invitations`); allowlist by default (nothing here is a module behind a flag: the kernel settings surface is always on, K-6 applies to `features` only); Arabic and English for every string.
- ADR-0018 "Decision" (one `sites` table, `kind ∈ {branch, warehouse, both}`, National Address short code, point, opening hours, staff assignments through `site_ids`). ADR-0025 (tenancy). ADR-0006 (nothing provisioned on first use: this order creates no account; PLAT-13 does). ADR-0031 (identity tables on the platform pool: memberships and invitations are tenant tables on the app pool, users are platform-pool only; a member door that needs both runs the identity part through PLAT-11's `IdentityStore` and the tenant part through the app unit of work, see 2.9).

## 5. Exit criteria

- Every operation of the `account`, `members` and `sites` tags answers per its contract with the contract's error codes and no other.
- No door takes an account id from a request; a foreign id is `404`; a site-scoped principal cannot read or change a site outside its `site_ids`.
- Only an owner grants, removes or revokes `owner`; the last active owner cannot be removed; nobody changes or revokes their own membership.
- A role change or revocation ends the member's sessions of that membership; a second-factor reset ends every session and mails a link.
- The site limit holds under concurrent creates; a registered in-use check refuses deactivation and kind changes.
- Every write is idempotent and audited with before and after in the same transaction.
- The adversary pass finds no critical or high.

## 6. Not in this order

Account provisioning, name and limit changes, suspension and closure (PLAT-13: `platformProvisionAccount`, `platformUpdateAccount`, `platformSuspendAccount`, `platformCloseAccount`; the account-name bound of PLAT-9 L-7 applies there); platform staff management (PLAT-13); feature flags and `features` in `Me` (PLAT-13, K-6); driver memberships, `drivers` usage and the drivers resource (DEL-4); API keys and their usage (wave 4); the order counter's increments (DEL-2); the in-use checks themselves (DEL-2, DEL-8, WMS-2 register them); custom roles; the web screens (WEB-3); `platformListAccountMembers` (PLAT-13 cross-tenant read); per-account export and deletion (wave 1 data order).

Leg 1 (items A to D):

1. **Migration 0014** is drizzle-kit's output from `schema/accounts.ts` and the new `schema/sites.ts`, with the SRID added by hand (drizzle-kit 0.31.11 writes `geometry(point)` for `geometry('point', { srid: 4326 })`; the snapshot keeps its spelling, so a later generate sees no drift) and the policies and the `elx_platform` revoke appended. `core_accounts.provisioned_by` is a foreign key to `core_users` (the schema files import each other lazily, as every `references()` is a thunk). Extra CHECKs beyond 2.1's list: `core_accounts.version >= 1`, `core_sites.version >= 1`. `core_usage_counters` has no `id` (its key is the primary key of 2.1); `core_sites.address_text` is nullable (the contract's field is optional).
2. **Grants as 2.1 reads them:** `elx_platform` keeps its writes on `core_accounts` and loses `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE` on `core_sites` and `core_usage_counters` ("read on all three"); PLAT-13's console site doors (`platformProvisionSite`) will grant what they need. `elx_app` keeps the default four on the two tenant tables, `DELETE` included ("CRUD"), although no door deletes a site (K-5: deactivated, never deleted once referenced).
3. **Which pool runs the account doors (2.9's rule applied to 2.2, 2.3):** `core_accounts` is a platform table elx_app may only read (0003, review M-3; the schema test forbids an elx_app write), so `getAccount`, `getAccountSettings` and `updateAccountSettings` read and write the row on the platform pool through `platform/accounts.repository.ts` (`AccountRecords`, `accountStoreOnPool`), always for `getContext().accountId`; usage (`core_sites`, `core_usage_counters`) is read in the request's app unit of work. The request holds its app connection while it waits for a platform one; the platform transaction never waits on the app pool, so no wait cycle can form between the pools as long as the platform side takes no lock the app side holds (see #4). The settings audit row is written by `AuditSink.record` inside that platform transaction (K-7: the row and the change commit together); the idempotency key stays in the app transaction, so a crash between the two commits re-applies the same patch on retry, which then changes nothing and writes nothing (#6).
4. **`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, not `FOR UPDATE` (deviation from 2.3's wording):** the request's own app transaction already holds `FOR KEY SHARE` on the account row (the idempotency reservation's foreign key to `core_accounts`), which `FOR UPDATE` waits for; on the other pool that wait never ends (the red run of B: every settings write timed out). `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` still orders concurrent settings writers and does not conflict with key-share locks; the `UPDATE` touches no key column. Any later door that updates `core_accounts` on the platform pool inside a tenant request must do the same (memory `api/core`).
5. **PATCH presence:** the generated `UpdateAccountSettingsBody` fills `max_attempts`, `allowed_cod_methods` and `offline_window_hours` with their contract defaults when absent, so the parsed body cannot tell "sent" from "absent". The kernel's `ContractPipe` now records the top-level fields of a JSON object body per parsed input (`sentBodyFields(input)`, a `WeakMap`, so `ContractInput`'s shape and every existing test stay as they were); the merge takes only those. Every field named there passed the strict schema.
6. **Settings semantics:** `before` and `after` hold the effective values (defaults applied) of the keys whose value changed; a patch that changes nothing writes nothing (no version, no audit row) and answers the current settings. The stored document holds only keys ever sent (no defaults); `effectiveSettings` lays it over the defaults on every read, nested `proof_policy` and `auto_dispatch` key by key. A stored document the contract does not allow fails the response parse (500) rather than being shown.
7. **Field codes:** the reason-code rules answer `invalid` (count over 100, code pattern, repeated code, label with a line break: CR, LF, NEL, U+2028, U+2029), `too_short` (empty label) and `too_long` (over 80 characters, counted as code points); the COD gate answers the new field code `owner_gate` ("This option is not available yet." / "هذا الخيار غير متاح بعد.", `packages/i18n` `fields`), as 2.3 names it.
8. **`getAccount` presentation:** `country` and `timezone` are presented as the literals the CHECKs allow; `provisioned_by` is omitted while null (the contract types it as a non-nullable UUID); the audit rows of the account doors carry the request facts of PLAT-11's `@AuthRequest()` (address, agent, client request id). `ScopedRepository` gained `countWhere` (packages/db) for the usage and limit counts.
9. **The list page helper (2.4):** `modules/kernel/contract/list-page.ts` holds the signed cursor codec (moved from `audit/cursor.ts`, same payload, HMAC key and 24 h lifetime), `planListPage` (documented sort fields with their kind, `-` prefix, default, binding to operation, reader, filters and sort), `presentPage`, `keysetAfter`/`keysetOrder` on (sort value, `id`) and `enumListFilter` for `status`-style comma lists. A cursor's key is now any sort value up to 1,024 characters; the planner checks a time key's form for time sorts (it was the codec's check). The three audit doors use it through `auditListSorts`, `auditPageQuery` and `auditKeyOf` in `audit-log.query.ts`; `audit-page.ts` is gone; `audit/cursor.ts` re-exports the codec under its PLAT-7 names, and `encode` still takes PLAT-7's `{ occurredAt, id }` key, so the audit tests import and call it unchanged. `platformListOutbox` keeps its own `id` keyset (PLAT-8) and only imports the codec from its new home. The `q` filter and its escaping arrive with `listMembers` in leg 2.
10. **`created_at` keys at millisecond precision:** a row's `created_at` reaches the service as a `Date` (milliseconds), so the sites list orders and compares `date_trunc('milliseconds', created_at)` with `id` as the tie-breaker; comparing the raw microsecond column with a millisecond cursor would skip rows of the same millisecond (the mutation red). No index serves this order; an account has few sites. The first version of the test planted the five times in five statements (five `now()`s, flaky 2 in 6); it now plants them in one.
11. **Contract change (deviation from §0 "packages/contracts scenarios only"):** the generated strict body of `createSite` and `updateSite` required `id` and `created_at`, because Orval's Zod output keeps the fields a body inherits through `allOf` from `Base` (it drops only a schema's own `readOnly` fields). 2.5 reads "the `Site` body less the read-only fields", so: `Base`'s `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` are `readOnly: true` in `components.yaml` (they were never input), and `scripts/operations.ts` omits a body's inherited `readOnly` fields (`inheritedReadOnlyBodyFields`; generation fails if such a body is not an object schema). It changes the request schemas of 19 operations, all creates and updates of `Base` resources (staff: sites, zones, vehicles, slot days, slots, locations, products, suppliers, purchase orders; platform: `platformProvisionSite`, `platformProvisionZone`), none implemented before this order; the generated client's input types lose the same fields (`NonReadonly`); responses are unchanged. `activateSite`'s 422 description now names `limit_exceeded` (#13). The lead should confirm the contract change; reverting it leaves `createSite` unusable without a client-sent id.
12. **The site write lock:** 2.5's "lock on the account row" cannot be taken in the tenant unit of work (elx_app has no `UPDATE` on `core_accounts`, so no `FOR UPDATE` or `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`), and a platform-pool lock would not outlive the platform transaction. The limit itself is read on the platform pool without a lock.

Leg 2 (items E to H):

19. **Where the member doors live:** `modules/kernel/members/` (controller, service, tenant repository, presenter), registered by `AuthModule.forRoot` rather than `KernelModule`: they delegate to `Invitations` and `SecondFactorResets`, which `AuthModule` builds from the config, and `KernelModule` imports no dynamic module. 20. **Pools per door (2.9):** `listMembers` and `getMember` read memberships in the request's tenant unit of work and their users on the platform pool (`UsersRepository.findByIds`, PLAT-11's identity store, read only, no lock). `inviteMember` reads the sites, the caller's membership and the invitee's existing membership in the tenant unit of work (no lock), the invitee's user by email and the account's `default_locale` on the platform pool (no lock), then runs `Invitations.createStaff` (its own platform transaction: it locks the membership row `FOR UPDATE`, which the request's app transaction never holds, since the invite reads memberships unlocked) and `recordCommitted`. So the request holds its app connection while it waits for a platform one, and no platform transaction of the door waits on the request's app transaction (PLAT-11 8.2 #29, 8.2 #3 here).
21. **`listMembers` pages in memory:** the `name` sort and `q` need the users, which elx_app cannot read, and a membership page cannot be cut in SQL by a column of another pool. The door reads every membership of the account the `status` and `site_id` filters keep (SQL, RLS), their users on the platform pool with `q` applied there (`ILIKE` over name and email, the text escaped by `containsPattern`, so `%`, `_` and `\` match literally), then orders by (sort value, `id`) and cuts after the cursor in memory (`keysetSlice` in `contract/list-page.ts`, UTF-16 code-unit order, so names do not follow the database collation as `listSites` does). The cursor is PLAT-7's (signed, bound to the operation, the account, the filters with the trimmed `q`, and the sort); `q` blank after trimming is no filter. Cost: every page reads the account's memberships, fine for a staff list; when DEL-4 puts driver memberships in the same table, a platform-pool keyset query with the token's account as an explicit predicate would page in SQL instead. Every state is listed (2.8), driver memberships too once they exist.
22. **The invited `name`** is 1 to 120 characters on one line (the site-name rule of 2.5 through `oneLineTextProblem`); the contract leaves it unbounded. For an existing user `createStaff` keeps the stored name (PLAT-11 8.2 #22). `email` is lower-cased for the lookup, the audit row and the call. `roles` and `site_ids` are deduplicated in order; `driver` is refused by the generated schema (`StaffRole`), at `/body/roles/<i>`.
23. **`site_ids`** must name sites of the account, active or inactive; the first that does not (another account's included) is `400 validation_failed` at `/body/site_ids/<i>` with code `invalid`, never `404` (2.7). The test that pages members newest first plants their `created_at` one second apart: rows of one millisecond order by `id`, and UUIDv7 ids minted by the test in one millisecond are not monotonic.
24. **The owner rule at the invitation** reads the caller's membership as stored, in the tenant transaction (a role removed since the token was minted no longer counts; a caller whose membership is no longer `active` is `401 token_invalid`). Inviting with `owner` needs an owner; so does replacing a pending (`invited`) membership that holds `owner` (staff.yaml: "grants or removes the owner role"); a revoked membership's old roles do not count. An `active` membership is `createStaff`'s `409 invalid_transition` (`details.current_status`), passed through.
25. **The invitation's language** is the account's `default_locale` (0014): `InviteMemberRequest` carries none, and the mail goes out before the invitee has a locale of their own. The test sets it per account and receives the `ar` and the `en` mail through Mailpit, then accepts the link.
