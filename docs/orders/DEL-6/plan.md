## 0. Goal

A dispatcher offers an accepted order to up to twenty eligible drivers with an expiry, or assigns it to one directly, and the first driver to accept wins while the other offers are withdrawn; the driver sees offers and the run on a stream and in the app's lists, accepts or declines, and loses an expired offer; an account with auto-dispatch on offers each accepted order in waves without a dispatcher; capacity counters refuse a driver's eleventh parcel and a cash-blocked driver gets nothing; a dispatcher unassigns or reassigns with an `If-Match` and the driver is told; the dispatch board and the driver app receive every order, offer, shift and position change over server-sent events with a replay buffer and a polling twin, so that DEL-7 can record field events on a known assignee and WEB-7 can draw the board live.

### 2a. Lead's improvements over the roadmap row

- **Adversary on** despite the roadmap's "no": streams are a new data path to staff and drivers and the driver doors change assignments.
- **Eligibility in one place with typed reasons** (2.2), reused by the manual doors, the driver's accept and auto-dispatch, so WEB-7 and DRV-4 can say why.
- **Transfer is an assignment change, not a status change** (2.3), as §3.13 says; `picked_up` orders cannot be unassigned because the parcel is physical.
- **Auto-dispatch stops when a dispatcher acts** (2.5) and gives up after three waves with an audit trail, so a board never shows an order being offered forever.
- **One stream buffer consumer** (2.6) fed by the outbox, so a door never writes to Redis and a lost Redis costs the live fan-out only, as data §6 states.
- **The stream filters as data with a test per reader** (2.7) are the visibility rule the adversary checks.

## 5. Exit criteria

- An offer to several drivers ends with exactly one assignment, the losers withdrawn, the counters right, and the order `assigned`; expiry and decline return the order to `accepted`.
- A direct assignment, a transfer and an unassignment move the counters and the assignment rows as D-9 and D-10 say; a `picked_up` order is never unassigned.
- Auto-dispatch offers in waves and stops after three or when a dispatcher acts.
- A dispatcher's board and a driver's app receive their events live, replay a gap by `Last-Event-ID`, and resync past the buffer; a site-scoped member and a driver see only what README §6 allows; a stream closes at token expiry and on revocation.
- No stream or push payload carries recipient data; both RLS lanes green; the contract is unchanged.
