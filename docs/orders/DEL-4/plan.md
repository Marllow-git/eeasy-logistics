## 0. Goal

A dispatcher creates a driver by phone and the driver receives the app invitation over WhatsApp, signs in to the driver app with a one-time code bound to the device, keeps a 90-day device-bound refresh token whose reuse revokes the family, sets a four-to-six-digit cash PIN after the first sign-in and verifies it online when the device needs a fresh verifier, while the dispatcher lists drivers and vehicles, changes a driver's home site, status and capacity, revokes a lost phone and unlocks a PIN after five failures, every rule audited and budgeted, so that DEL-5 and DRV-1 build shifts and the app on a driver identity that exists (identity §3.2, §4; domain model §3.5; ADR-0034).

## 5. Exit criteria

- A driver created by a dispatcher receives the WhatsApp invitation and signs in on at most two devices with a code; a third device is refused; a revoked device cannot refresh and its tokens die.
- Every door answers the same body to a stranger's phone, a disabled user and a suspended driver; the cost of a verify is the same for each.
- The cash PIN is Argon2id, locks after five failures, unlocks only through the dispatcher's reset, and never appears in a log, audit row, idempotency store or response.
- A refresh reused outside the grace revokes the family; archive revokes every device and clears push tokens.
- The adversary pass finds no critical or high.
