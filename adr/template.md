# ADR-NNNN: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN | deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** owner | architecture lead
- **Source:** where the decision came from (owner ruling, UAT finding, teardown, prior ADR)

## Context

What situation forces a decision. Facts only, two to six sentences.

## Decision

What we do, stated so that a reviewer can tell whether a PR obeys it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now also do.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative in one or two sentences, and why it lost.
